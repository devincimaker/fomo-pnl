fomo all-time PnL

A bookmark that shows anyone's all-time PnL right on fomo.family, using your existing fomo login.

fomo PnL
  1. Drag the purple button onto your bookmarks bar (Cmd+Shift+B shows it).
  2. Go to fomo.family while logged in.
  3. Click the bookmark. On someone's profile page it looks them up automatically; anywhere else, type a handle.

If it says the login expired, reload the fomo page and click again.
